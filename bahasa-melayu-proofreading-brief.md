# Bahasa Melayu Malaysia — Proofreading Reference Brief

Reference for proofreading the Malay-language strings of a school web application
(target: Malaysian standard Malay, not Indonesian).
Prepared from DBP, KPM and encyclopaedic sources; see **Sources** at the end.

---

## 0. Scope, premises and assumptions (read first)

| Premise in the brief request | What the sources actually show |
|---|---|
| "Ejaan Bahasa Melayu, 2019" / "Perpandan" spelling reform | **No 2019 spelling reform was found in any DBP or KPM source.** The in-force standard is DBP's **_Pedoman Umum Ejaan Bahasa Melayu_** (first issued 1985; DBP reprints it — KPM's own bibliography cites the **2016** DBP edition). The title is *Pedoman Umum Ejaan Bahasa Melayu*, not "Perpanduan/Perpandan". 2019 in KPM materials refers to the *DSKP Bahasa Melayu Sekolah Menengah* (KPM, 2019), not to spelling. → Proofread against **Pedoman Umum Ejaan Bahasa Melayu** + **Kamus Dewan Edisi Keempat (2015)** + **Tatabahasa Dewan (ed. ketiga, 2015)**. |
| "dan" vs "dan" | Treated as the **comma-before-"dan" rule** (serial/Oxford comma), which is what DBP actually rules on. |
| "pinyin of -tuan/-an" | Treated as **the writing (ejaan) of words ending in -an/-tuan** (kesatuan, perpaduan, pemerintahan) plus number+suffix (50-an) and partial reduplication (bunga-bungaan). "Pinyin" has no meaning in Malay orthography. |
| "Pentaksian" | KPM's standard term is **_Pentaksiran_** (PBD = Pentaksiran Bilik Darjah). "Pentaksian" is a non-standard form — do not use it. |

Verification status used below: **[V]** = verified verbatim in a DBP/KPM source during this
research; **[S]** = standard practice reported by secondary/reputable sources; **[?]** = flagged,
verify in PRPM (prpm.dbp.gov.my) before shipping.

## 1. Orthography and spelling (Pedoman Umum Ejaan Bahasa Melayu, DBP)

### 1.1 The standard

* **Pedoman Umum Ejaan Bahasa Melayu** (DBP) — governs: abjad, vokal akhir, huruf besar,
  penulisan kata (dasar/terbitan/gandaan/kata majemuk), imbuhan + garis hubung, angka,
  unsur serapan, tanda baca. **[V]**
* **Kamus Dewan Edisi Keempat** (DBP, 2015) — word existence, sense, hyphenation.
  Lookup tool: **PRPM** (prpm.dbp.gov.my) — also exposes *Istilah MABBIM* and *Kamus
  Teknologi Maklumat*.
* **Tatabahasa Dewan, ed. ketiga** (Nik Safiah Karim et al., DBP, 2015) — grammar. **[V]**
  (cited in KPM's own reference list, sites.google.com/moe-dl.edu.my/sisemejaanbahasamelayu)
* **Garis Panduan Ejaan Rumi Bahasa Melayu (ed. semakan)** (DBP, 2021) — Rumi/Jawi. **[V]**

### 1.2 Concrete, checkable rules

| # | Rule | Correct | Wrong / avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **baru vs baharu** — *baru* = "just/recently" (time adverb); *baharu* = "new" (adjective). DBP also accepts *baru* for "new", so *baru* is not an error there, but *baharu* is the precise choice and the only choice when the contrast matters. Derivatives always use *baharu*: pembaharuan, memperbaharui, kebaharuan. Fixed place name: **Johor Bahru**. | "Saya **baru** sampai." / "telefon **baharu** itu" / "kerajaan **baharu**" | "saya **baharu** sampai" (wrong meaning); "**baru** peraturan" when "new" is meant (ambiguous) **[S]** |
| 2 | **kerana vs karena** — *kerana* is the Malaysian spelling. *Karena* is **Indonesian** and must never appear in MY copy (except in quoted Indonesian text). | "Gagal sebab **kerana** sambungan" → better: "Gagal **kerana** sambungan" | "**karena** sambungan" **[V]** (Wikipedia MY/ID tables) |
| 3 | **oleh kerana → oleh sebab** — DBP: *oleh* is a kata depan and must be followed by a kata nama; *kerana* is a kata hubung. | "**Oleh sebab** jumlah kemalangan meningkat, pihak polis mengadakan operasi…" | "**Oleh kerana** jumlah kemalangan…" **[V]** (DBP *Pedoman Bahasa*) |
| 4 | **Serial "dan"** — for two short coordinated items no comma before *dan*; a comma before *dan* is permitted (not wrong) when the list is long or the comma prevents ambiguity; with two parallel full clauses, the comma is *not* used unless needed for clarity. Never write "&" in Malay copy. | "Ali membeli buku, pen **dan** dakwat." / "A, B, **dan** C" (long list, clarity) | "Ali membeli buku **dan** pen **dan** dakwat." (run-on); "**&**" in prose **[V]** (DBP *Pedoman Bahasa*) |
| 5 | **-kan / -i suffixes** — *-kan* forms a transitive/causative verb (make/become/for someone): *wartakan, besarkan, tetapkan, lancarkan*, and the passive *di-…-kan* (dilancarkan). *-i* marks an action directed at a place or a particular target/part: *masuki, tulisi, hadiri*. Both attach **straight onto the root with no hyphen**. If unsure which verb exists, look it up in PRPM — do not coin forms. | "Fail itu dipadam**kan**." / "Beliau menghadiri (hadir**i**) mesyuarat." | "dipadam-kan", "hadir-kan mesyuarat", "memasuk**kan** ke dalam" (double-marking: *memasukkan ke dalam* is redundant — use *memasukkan* or *masuk ke dalam*) **[S]** (Tatabahasa Dewan) |
| 6 | **Kata majemuk (rangkai kata) = separate words**; bound (terikat) elements = one word. | separate: *mata pelajaran, meja tulis, guru besar, tingkah laku, orang ramai, sahaja?* → *mata pelajaran, guru besar, tingkah laku*; one word: *prasekolah, prasarana, infrastruktur, prasarangka, perbuatan, matahari, peribahasa, sekaligus, daripada, kepada, apabila, barangkali, padahal* | "mata-pelajaran", "guru-besar", "prasarana/infrastruktur terbitan" split, "prase kolah" **[V]** (Pedoman, Bab III D) |
| 7 | **Imbuhan attach to the immediately adjacent element of a compound** (no hyphen). | *sebar**luas**kan, ber**ibu** angkat, mem**per**baharui, menyebarluas, penghancur-lebur?* → Pedoman examples: *sebarluaskan, beribu-angkatkan?* → use *sebar luas kan* variant only per Pedoman: **sebarluaskan** | "ber-ibu angkat", "sebar-luas-kan" **[V]** (Pedoman, Bab III B.2–3) |
| 8 | **-an / -tuan type endings are written as one word** (no hyphen). | *kesatuan, persekutuan, perpaduan, kemakmuran, pemerintahan, kewangan, pentadbiran* | "kesatu-an", "persekutu-an" **[V]** (imbuhan/akhiran serangkai rule) |
| 9 | **Hyphen is used with: full reduplication, numbers + -an, se- + capitalised word, bound element + capitalised word, acronym + imbuhan.** | *guru-guru, murid-murid, ciri-ciri, kanak-kanak, undang-undang, tahun 50-an, ke-2, se-Malaysia, se-Tanah Melayu, pro-Malaysia, anti-COVID-19, KDN-nya* | "guru guru", "ciri ciri", "tahun 50an", "seMalaysia" **[V]** (Pedoman, Bab V F) |
| 10 | **Capitals — first word of sentence; direct-speech quote.** | "**S**impan perubahan." | "simpan perubahan." **[V]** (Pedoman, Bab II A.1–2) |
| 11 | **Languages, ethnic groups, nationalities are capitalised — but *bahasa* itself is lower-case when used as a common noun.** | *bahasa Melayu, bahasa Indonesia, orang Inggeris, bangsa Melayu, suku Kadazan* | "**B**ahasa melayu"; "bahasa **m**elayu" **[V]** (Pedoman, Bab II A.7) |
| 12 | **Months, days, public holidays, historic events are capitalised.** | *bulan Ogos, hari Jumaat, 31 Ogos 1957, Hari Kebangsaan, Tahun Baharu Cina* | "31 ogos 1957", "hari isnin" **[V]** (Pedoman, Bab II A.8) |
| 13 | **Official bodies / laws / documents are capitalised, except linking words (dan, untuk, yang, di, ke…).** | *Kementerian Pendidikan Malaysia, Dewan Rakyat, Lembaga Urusan **dan** Tabung Haji, Perisytiharan Kuala Lumpur* | "**d**ewan rakyat"; "Lembaga Urusan **Dan** Tabung Haji" **[V]** (Pedoman, Bab II A.10, A.11) |
| 14 | **Job/title words are capitalised only with a person's name (or as a form of address); otherwise lower-case.** | "Guru Besar Ahmad Ali"; "Yang Berusaha **Guru Besar**" (salutation, DBP allows capital) vs "guru besar sekolah itu" | "**G**uru Besar yang bertugas"; "Tuan pengerusi majlis" (generic → lower) **[V]** (Pedoman A.5; DBP *Pedoman Bahasa* Q1) |
| 15 | **Names of months/days/holidays/geography/place/institution names capitalised**; generic nouns not. | *Selat Melaka, Sungai Pahang, sekolah kebangsaan* (generic), *Sekolah Kebangsaan Bukit Nenas* (name) | "**s**elat melaka"; "ke arah **T**imur" (generic direction) **[V]** (Pedoman A.9) |
| 16 | **Book/periodical/programme titles: every word capitalised except trailing particles (di, ke, dari, untuk, yang).** | *Dari Ave Mari ke Jalan Lain ke Roma; Pelajaran Ekonomi untuk Sekolah-Sekolah Menengah* | capitalising a mid-title "yang/untuk" **[V]** (Pedoman A.11–12) |
| 17 | **Loanwords: fully absorbed words use Malay spelling; not-yet-absorbed terms keep source spelling.** | *institut* (not institiut), *unit* (not yunit), *struktur, fokus, karbon, teknik, sistem, senario, klasik, vaksin, universiti*; keep source form for recent/unabsorbed terms | "institiut", "yunit", "straktur", "fokas" **[V]** (Pedoman, Bab I B.1.5 + Bab IV) |
| 18 | **Numbers**: digits for measurements/time/money/quantities; large numbers may be written fully; a number at the start of a sentence is written in words. Thousands separator = comma; **decimal mark = point**; years carry no separator. | *5 kilogram, 9.30 pagi, RM5,000.00, tahun 1957, 12 juta orang; "Lima belas orang terkorban…"* | "5,5 kg" (Indonesian decimal comma), "tahun 1,957", sentence-initial "15 orang…" **[V]** (Pedoman Bab III; en-wiki MY/ID decimal tables) |
| 19 | **Abbreviations/acronyms: no full stops** for body/agency initials and accepted acronyms; full stops only for honorifics (*Dr., Encik, Pn., Prof.*) and a few lexicalised abbreviations (*dsb., dll., hlm.*). | *RPH, KPM, DBP, DSKP, PBD, PdP; Dr. Ahmad* | "R.P.H.", "K.P.M." **[V]** (Pedoman Bab V A.3–4, A.9) |
| 20 | **Tanda pisah (–) for ranges = two hyphens wide, no inner spaces; elipsis = three dots with spaces, only for omission/unfinished thought; decimal point; thousands comma; no comma inside years/page numbers.** | *1910–1954, Taiping–Seremban, "Kalau begitu … ya, marilah"* | "1910 - 1954", "1910 - 1954", "1910 – 1954" (spaced), "…" used to end a sentence for style, "1.954" as a year **[V]** (Pedoman Bab V F, G, B.14) |
| 21 | **Apostrophe** exists only as the elision mark (tanda penyingkat) and is a curly **’**; Malay has **no possessive ’s**. | *Dato’ Sri, Ali’kan (= akan), buku Ali itu / buku Ali* | "Dato'", "buku Ali's", "'kan" **[V]** (Pedoman Bab V P; British-style ’ per DBP typography) |

## 4. Common grammar errors that stand out to a Malaysian reader

| # | Error class | ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"yang" + preposition** (double relative) | "peranti **yang di mana** ia digunakan" | "peranti **yang** digunakan" | [S] Tatabahasa Dewan |
| 2 | **yang + ialah/adalah** | "murid **yang ialah** terbaik"; "Ini **adalah** guru" | "murid **yang** terbaik"; "Ini **ialah** guru" (**ialah** → noun; **adalah** → non-noun) | [S] Tatabahasa Dewan |
| 3 | Sentence-initial **"Adalah …"** | "**Adalah** dimaklumkan bahawa portal dibuka." | "**Dimaklumkan** bahawa portal dibuka." | [S] DBP usage guidance |
| 4 | **kata pemuja placement** (*sahaja, jua, juga, hanya, kira-kira*) | "**Sahaja** beliau hadir"; "kira-kira selesai **kira-kira**" | "Beliau **sahaja** hadir"; "**kira-kira** 500 murid" (approximation pemuja goes before the number; *sahaja/jua/juga* go immediately after the emphasised element). Avoid stacking *hanya … sahaja*. | [S] Tatabahasa Dewan |
| 5 | **kata bantu nama position** | "Menghantar **sudah**"; "Fail **akan dipadamkan boleh**" | Auxiliaries **precede** the verb in both voices: "**Sudah** menghantar", "**Sedang** memuat naik", "**Akan** menyimpan", "Fail itu **telah/boleh** dipadamkan" | [S] Tatabahasa Dewan |
| 6 | **sudah vs belum vs tidak** | "Portal **tidak** mengeluarkan sijil lagi" (for "hasn't yet") | "Portal **belum** mengeluarkan sijil" (*belum* = not yet; *tidak* = does not) | [S] Tatabahasa Dewan |
| 7 | **Clumsy passive (di-)** | "Pesalah itu **dijatuhkan** hukuman gantung" | "**Dijatuhi** hukuman gantung" (or reverse: "Hukuman **dijatuhkan** kepada pesalah") | [V] DBP *Pedoman Bahasa* |
| 8 | **Wrong derivational verb** | "Cikgu mengajarkan Sejarah"; "Ibu **menghidangkan** rakan-rakan" | "Cikgu **mengajar** Sejarah" (*mengajarkan* needs a human object); "Ibu **menghidangi** rakan-rakan" | [V] DBP *Pedoman Bahasa* |
| 9 | **Redundancy (pleonasm)** | "berpindah-randah **dari satu tempat ke tempat lain**"; "**kemudahan** prasarana"; "**prasarana** dan kemudahan asas" in one phrase | "berpindah-randah" alone; "prasarana" alone (it already means facilities) | [V] DBP *Pedoman Bahasa* |
| 10 | **Non-existent prefix** | "**mer**bahaya" | "**ber**bahaya" (Malay has no *mer-* prefix) | [V] DBP *Pedoman Bahasa* |
| 11 | **English verbs in buttons** | "Download", "Upload", "Delete", "Edit", "Save", "Search", "Submit", "Login" | "Muat turun", "Muat naik", "Padam", "Sunting", "Simpan", "Cari", "Hantar", "Log masuk" | [S] DBP IT glossary practice |
| 12 | **English nouns in prose** | "…fail disimpan dalam **server** dan dibuka melalui **browser**…" | "…fail disimpan dalam **pelayan** dan dibuka melalui **pelayar**…" | [V] PRPM |
| 13 | **Serial lists** | "buku **&** pen"; "A, B, C **,** dan D" ambiguity unresolved | Use **dan** (never &); comma before *dan* only when needed for clarity (see §1 rule 4) | [V] DBP *Pedoman Bahasa* |
| 14 | **Apostrophe misuse** | "buku **Ali's**"; "**Dato'** Sri"; straight quote ' | Malay has no possessive ’s → "buku Ali itu / buku Ali"; elision uses curly **’**: "**Dato’** Sri" | [V] Pedoman Bab V P |
| 15 | **– vs ...** | "1 Julai **–** 15 Julai"; "Sila tunggu**....**"; sentence ended with "…" | "1 Julai**–**15 Julai" (en dash, no spaces); "…" only for omission/truncation — UI may use "…" for cut-off text, never as decorative full stop | [V] Pedoman Bab V F, G |
| 16 | **Plural reduplication** | "**5 orang murid-murid**"; "semua **kakitangan-kakitangan**" | Numbers/determiners take the **singular**: "5 orang murid"; collectives stay singular: "semua kakitangan"; use reduplication for non-specific plurals: "**ciri-ciri** sistem ini", "**guru-guru** yang hadir" | [V] Pedoman Bab III C |
| 17 | **Double marking** | "memasukkan **ke dalam** kolum" | "memasukkan ke kolum" or "masuk ke dalam kolum" | [S] Tatabahasa Dewan |
| 18 | **meN- allomorph typos** | "mesimpan", "menbaca", "mengambil"✅ vs "menyapu"❌ as "mesapu" | b→m (*membaca*), t→n (*menulis*), c→ny (*menyimpan*), d→n (*mendapat*), g→ng (*mengemas*), s→y (*menyemak*), j→ny? (no: j→ny does not occur; *menjimatkan* keeps j) | [S] Tatabahasa Dewan |

### Where English (or an English-looking term) is acceptable in Malaysian tech UI

* **Already-standard Malay items that look English:** *log masuk, log keluar, kata laluan, fail,
  pelayan, pelayar, e-mel, sistem, komputer, internet, templat, arkib, digital, aplikasi,
  paparan?* — these are dictionary/standard terms, not anglicisms. ✅
* **Keep as-is:** brand/product names (Google Classroom, WhatsApp, DELIMa, MySchool?), file
  formats and tech acronyms (**PDF, JPG, URL, API, PIN, ID, SMS, ICT**), and unabsorbed technical
  terms (DBP rule: keep source spelling until absorbed). ✅
* **Do not anglicise:** any verb or noun with an established Malay equivalent (table above). ❌
* *Dalam talian* is the DBP term for "online", but "online" in UI chrome is common in MY apps —
  a style choice, not an error; be consistent. **[S]**

## 5. Capitalisation conventions in Malaysian UI copy

| Element | Convention | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Headings / page titles** | **Sentence case** (first word + proper nouns) is the Malaysian default in government and editorial practice; Title Case appears mainly in KPM/KPM-style **form labels** | "Maklumat murid", "Semakan Rancangan Pengajaran Harian" |
| **Buttons / field labels** | Sentence case in general UI; **Title Case when mirroring an official KPM form** (then *dan* stays lower-case) | "Simpan perubahan" vs label "Standard Kandungan" / "Strategi Pengajaran **dan** Pembelajaran" |
| **Course/subject names** | **Capitalised** (they are proper names) | Bahasa Melayu, Bahasa Inggeris, Matematik, Sains, Sejarah, Pendidikan Islam, Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi (*dan* lower) |
| **Language name** | **M**elayu always capital; *bahasa* lower-case in running text per Pedoman; **Bahasa Melayu** (both capital) in titles, subject names, document names — KPM's own documents do this, so both are attested; match context | "kemahiran **bahasa** Melayu" (prose) / "**Bahasa Melayu Tahun 1**" (title) |
| **Months & days** | Always capital | 12 Mac 2026, hari Selasa, bulan Ogos |
| **Holidays / historic events** | Capital | Hari Guru, Hari Kebangsaan, Aidilfitri, Tahun Baharu Cina |
| **Proper nouns** | Capital — people, places, institutions, official documents, programme names | Sekolah Kebangsaan Taman Melati; Kementerian Pendidikan Malaysia; Dokumen Standard Kurikulum dan Pentaksiran |
| **Linking words inside names** | lower-case | Lembaga Urusan **dan** Tabung Haji; Pelajaran Ekonomi **untuk** Sekolah-Sekolah Menengah |
| **Job titles** | Capital only before a name / as form of address; else lower | "**Guru Besar** Noraini"; "guru besar itu"; "Yang Berusaha **Guru Besar**" |
| **Common nouns (murid, guru, kelas, sekolah, mata pelajaran, prasekolah)** | lower-case in prose | "50 murid hadir" |
| **Acronyms** | ALL CAPS, **no full stops**; full form + acronym in brackets at first mention | "Dokumen Standard Kurikulum dan Pentaksiran (DSKP)" → "DSKP" |
| **Religious terms** | Capital per Pedoman | Allah, Tuhan, Quran, Nabi, Islam (lower-case: haji, imam? — *haji* is lower-case when generic) |
| **Place/geography** | Capital (names); lower (generic features) | Selat Melaka; "menyeberangi selat" |

## 6. Practical checklist — 25 mechanically checkable rules

Run these against each string; every rule is phrased as "X must be Y".

1. **"karena"** must not occur anywhere (correct form: **kerana**), outside quoted Indonesian.
2. **"baru"** must mean "just/recently"; every string meaning "new" must use **baharu** (except the fixed name **Johor Bahru**).
3. **"oleh kerana"** must be **oleh sebab**.
4. **"&"** must not occur in Malay copy; lists must use **dan**.
5. A **comma before "dan"** must appear only in long/ambiguous lists; short two-item lists must have no comma before *dan*.
6. **Indonesian UI vocabulary** must not occur: *hapus, arsip, perangkat, pengaturan, kata sandi, peramban, tautan, berkas, unduh, unggah, mobil, kursi, uang, akun, beda, resmi, sehat, maret, agustus, senin, karena, karena?* → each must be replaced by the Malaysian form in §2.1.
7. **Settings** must be labelled **Tetapan** (never "Penetapan"/"Pengaturan"); **password** must be **Kata laluan**; **delete** must be **Padam**; **edit** must be **Sunting**; **upload/download** must be **Muat naik / Muat turun**.
8. **Compound nouns (rangkai kata)** must be written as separate words: *mata pelajaran, guru besar, tingkah laku, orang ramai* — never hyphenated.
9. **Bound/prefix compounds** must be written as one word: *prasekolah, prasarana, infrastruktur, perbuatan, kesatuan, persekutuan, perpaduan, pemerintahan* — never hyphenated or split.
10. **All reduplication** must use hyphens: *guru-guru, murid-murid, ciri-ciri, kanak-kanak, undang-undang* — never "guru guru".
11. **Numbers + suffix / se- + proper noun** must use a hyphen: *tahun 50-an, ke-2, se-Malaysia*.
12. **Nouns preceded by numerals or quantities** must be singular: "5 murid" must not be "5 orang murid-murid".
13. **Month and day names** must be capitalised and written Malaysian: *Mac, Ogos, Isnin, Ahad* — never *Maret, Agustus, Senin, Minggu*.
14. **Dates** must be written "31 Ogos 1957" (day–month–year, no ordinal suffix, no comma).
15. **Times** must be written "9.30 pagi" (point, not colon/comma, plus pagi/tengah hari/petang/malam).
16. **Decimals must use a point (3.5), thousands a comma (1,000), years no separator (2026)** — never Indonesian decimal commas.
17. **Acronyms** must be all caps with no full stops: *RPH, KPM, DBP, DSKP, KSSR, KSSM, PBD, PdP* — and must be expanded in brackets at first mention in long strings.
18. **Capitalisation of names**: *Kementerian Pendidikan Malaysia*, official bodies, subject names, holidays, months, places must be capitalised; *murid, guru, kelas, mata pelajaran, prasekolah* must be lower-case in prose; linking words (*dan, untuk, yang*) inside names must be lower-case.
19. **Job titles** must be lower-case unless immediately before a person's name or used as a salutation: "guru besar sekolah" ✅ / "Guru Besar Aminah" ✅ / "Guru Besar yang bertugas" ❌.
20. **Auxiliary verbs must precede the main verb** (*sudah/telah/sedang/akan/boleh + verb*), and **"belum" must be used (not "tidak")** for "not yet".
21. **"ialah" must be followed by a noun and "adalah" must not be**; no clause may contain both *ialah* and *adalah*.
22. **Passive and derived verbs must match their objects**: "dijatuhi hukuman" (not *dijatuhkan hukuman*), "mengajar Sejarah" (not *mengajarkan Sejarah*), "menghidangi tetamu" (not *menghidangkan tetamu*), "berbahaya" (not *merbahaya*).
23. **Possessives must use word order or -ku/-mu/-nya — never ’s**; elision apostrophes must be the curly **’** (*Dato’*, not *Dato'*).
24. **Ranges must use an en dash with no spaces (Tahun 1–3)**; ellipsis must be exactly three dots and only for omission/truncation — never as sentence punctuation.
25. **KPM strings must use official terms**: *Rancangan Pengajaran Harian* (not *Rancangan Pembelajaran/Pelajaran Harian*), **Pentaksiran** (not *Pentaksian*), *Standard Kandungan / Standard Pembelajaran / Objektif Pembelajaran / Kriteria Kejayaan / Refleksi / Aktiviti Pengayaan / Aktiviti Pemulihan*, *Tahun 1–6* and *Tingkatan 1–5* (not *Kelas 7*), *murid* for school learners (not *pelajar*), *Pembelajaran dan Pemudahcaraan (PdPc)* (not "Pedagogi dan Kaedah Pembelajaran" unless it matches the exact KPM form you are mirroring).
